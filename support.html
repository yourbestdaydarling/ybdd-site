Help with Your Best Day, Darling.

2026-10-27

When does the quote change?

At the start of a new calendar day in the device’s time zone. If the app was closed, the new quote appears when you open it again.

How do I change the language?

Tap the globe in the upper-right corner and choose English or Russian. The thought for the day stays the same.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Quotes, translations and source details are included in the app. You only need a connection if you choose to open an external page or email support. Delivery through messaging or email may require an internet connection.

A quote seems inaccurate or unsuitable

Open About this quote and include the author, the opening words and your chosen language in your email. We will check the source and translation. Catalog corrections are distributed with app updates; an older offline version cannot receive them automatically.

Something is not working

Describe what happened and, if convenient, include your iPhone model, iOS version and app version. Do not send passwords or personal documents. A screenshot is optional; please hide personal information first.

Sharing quotes

At your request, quote text or a PNG card with the YBDD logo is passed to the system share menu. Cards are created on your device. Settings and reading dates are not shared. You choose the app and recipient; the chosen service handles delivery under its own policies.

Support email retention

Support correspondence and attachments are deleted from the support mailbox 90 days after the issue is resolved, or earlier at the sender’s request. Email support to request deletion. The email provider’s own policy governs retention of its technical logs and backups.
