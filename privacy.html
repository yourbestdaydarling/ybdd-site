Privacy policy

Your Best Day, Darling (YBDD): a daily quote without an account.

2026-10-27

What the app collects

The app does not request personal information or send it to the developer. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, tracking, cloud sync or third-party SDKs. Quotes, translations and sources are bundled with the app and work offline.

What stays on your device

Your chosen language, calendar day, daily quote ID and shuffled quote order with its start date are stored locally to preserve your settings and one quote for the day. The app uses the device clock and time zone, not location services, to determine the new day. This information is not sent out of the app.

Deletion and backups

Deleting the app together with its data removes its local settings. System backups are governed by iOS settings, which the app does not manage.

External websites

Links to this policy and support open in an external browser only after you tap them. Literary source addresses are displayed as plain text in the app. When you choose to visit an external site, its own data policies apply.

Contacting support

You do not need to register or provide personal information to use the app. If you choose to email support, the recipient will receive your sender address, message and attachments. This information is used to respond to your request. Please avoid sending documents or sensitive information that is not needed to address your question. Support correspondence and attachments are deleted from the support mailbox 90 days after the issue is resolved, or earlier at the sender’s request. Email support to request deletion. The email provider’s own policy governs retention of its technical logs and backups.

This page

GitHub Pages is the selected website host. When the published site is visited, GitHub logs the visitor’s IP address for security, even without a signed-in account. The YBDD pages themselves contain no forms, cookies, analytics or advertising scripts, or external fonts. Proton Mail hosts the support mailbox; email providers also process information when you correspond with support. These services are separate from the offline app. Their policies are linked below.

Changes

This policy describes the current app version and will be updated if data practices change. For support or questions about information you voluntarily send, use the contact below.

Sharing quotes

At your request, quote text or a PNG card with the YBDD logo is passed to the system share menu. Cards are created on your device. Settings and reading dates are not shared. You choose the app and recipient; the chosen service handles delivery under its own policies.
